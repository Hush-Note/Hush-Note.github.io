The Soldier

Only a few manage to see my casual side. For most, I’m just the "Soldier." Can’t help it—that’s simply my nature. I don’t really know how to socialise the usual way. If someone shares a common space with me, that’s when it starts— they visit my room frequently, we play a few games together, and slowly, familiarity grows. Little by little, the barrier comes down, and eventually, they get to see the quirky side of me. There’s really no other way I know how to connect. The few who have managed to cross that barrier are rare— Jeevesh, Animesh, CP, Nihad, Aritra, and a handful of others. And sometimes I wonder… If she had just shown up a couple of times when I reached out— I was asking her to play or spend time with me, not asking her to join my friends or some group. I’m mostly by myself anyway. Even if she had hinted at joining her friends, I probably wouldn’t have. I wasn’t looking for a group. I was choosing her. Maybe if she had shown up, with a little more time and familiarity, the barrier would have come down. And perhaps **"Soldier"** would have become something else entirely.
