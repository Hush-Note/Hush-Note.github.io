Jealousy

Remember how jealous you became when a girl sat beside me during KD’s class? Even he made a light-hearted comment about it. I noticed it, but I never showed jealousy myself. That’s simply not something I do. I don’t ditch people behind their backs, question who they spend time with, or make a scene because someone has other friends. When something bothers me, I simply pull back and become distant. I know an extrovert will naturally have many people around them, and I never expected you to change that. But if you cared enough to feel jealous when someone else came close to me, why was it so difficult to understand what my efforts meant when I was the one trying to reach you? I wasn’t asking you to choose between people. I just wanted my efforts to be met halfway.

October 2, 2026

Transient Possibilities

Efforts mean nothing if they aren’t mutual. Feelings mean nothing if they aren’t mutual. Indirect behaviour means nothing without something direct to meet it halfway. I could sense the lies. Even those were ignored. Not because they didn’t matter, but because I still believed clear communication was all that was necessary. And perhaps that was okay. Possibilities decay like transients— even when the feelings that created them remain forever.

May 13, 2026

The Soldier

Only a few manage to see my casual side. For most, I’m just the "Soldier." Can’t help it—that’s simply my nature. I don’t really know how to socialise the usual way. If someone shares a common space with me, that’s when it starts— they visit my room frequently, we play a few games together, and slowly, familiarity grows. Little by little, the barrier comes down, and eventually, they get to see the quirky side of me. There’s really no other way I know how to connect. The few who have managed to cross that barrier are rare. And sometimes I wonder… If she had just shown up a couple of times when I reached out— I was asking her to play or spend time with me, not asking her to join my friends or some group. I’m mostly by myself anyway. Even if she had hinted at joining her friends, I probably wouldn’t have. I wasn’t looking for a group. I was choosing her. Maybe if she had shown up, with a little more time and familiarity, the barrier would have come down. And perhaps "Soldier" would have become something else entirely.
